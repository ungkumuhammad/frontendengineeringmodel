NH₃ → H₂ Cracker Design — Licensor KPIs

What this tool models

An ammonia cracker converts liquid/gaseous NH₃ back into hydrogen via the endothermic decomposition 2NH₃ → N₂ + 3H₂ (ΔH ≈ +92.4 kJ/mol at reaction conditions, or the ΔH⁰ ≈ 46 kJ/mol NH₃ basis some licensors quote — the two bases are not interchangeable). The cracked gas is then purified — ammonia recovery (H₂O/NH₃ absorption-distillation) and PSA — to 99.5–99.999 %wt H₂ depending on licensor.

Sizing a cracker is the demand side of an ammonia receiving/storage terminal: every tonne of H₂ the cracker must deliver requires several tonnes of NH₃ feed (the NH₃:H₂ mass ratio below, which varies by licensor and fuel mode). Use “🔗 Link model to storage” below to send that required NH₃ throughput straight into the NH₃ Storage Design module's demand input, so the terminal is sized against the cracker's actual offtake rather than a manually re-typed number.

Choose a commercial route below: Own & Operate compares a licensed unit's CAPEX, OPEX and LCOH by licensor case (KBR H2ACT at 4 capacities × 3 ammonia price points; Duiker AHC's lump-sum CAPEX/OPEX; Casale MACH2, technical-only, no cost data); or Tolling / offtake compares indicative storage-and-cracking-as-a-service tariffs (Vopak/Linde Antwerp LoI, VTTI Rotterdam/Antwerp Amplifhy) where you never own the CAPEX.

Clean-molecule certification — indicative only

A cracker's fuel mode drives its direct carbon intensity: a 100% clean-fuel (NH₃/H₂ off-gas fired) cracker can be CI ≈ 0 direct, while a 100% natural-gas-fired cracker adds materially (e.g. KBR H₂ACT 100% NG ≈ 0.80–0.81 kgCO₂/kgH₂ direct, per its own KPI table). This stacks with upstream ammonia production CI.

Two frameworks are commonly referenced: EU RFNBO (RED II/III, ≥70% GHG reduction vs. fossil comparator, ≈28.2 gCO₂e/MJ derived ceiling, verified via ISCC EU/CertifHy) and Korea's KEEI clean hydrogen scheme (proposed threshold CI < 4.0 kgCO₂/kgH₂). The two use different bases (gCO₂e/MJ vs. kgCO₂/kgH₂; LHV H₂ ≈ 120 MJ/kg → 1 kgCO₂/kgH₂ ≈ 8.33 gCO₂/MJ) — never compare them without converting first.

This tool states what an indicative CI figure would/would not clear against a threshold — it never asserts that a project "qualifies" as RFNBO or KEEI-clean; that is a certification outcome subject to full additionality/correlation/verification.

No-fabrication note

All licensor and tolling figures above (NH₃:H₂ ratio, energy efficiency, H₂ purity, CI, CAPEX, OPEX, LCOH, tolling tariffs) are carried over verbatim from the corresponding technical or commercial package in the Gentari-ammoniacracker repository's Licensor/ and tolling/ folders and its tools/cracker_model/data.py single source-of-truth, each with its own citation and revision date (see the source line under each result). Figures marked "technical-only" or "not disclosed" (Casale CAPEX/OPEX) are intentionally left blank rather than estimated. The one non-cited figure is KBR's OSBL = 55% of ISBL, explicitly flagged as an assumption (confirmed only at the 12 ktpa point, applied flat elsewhere for lack of a second data point). All cost figures are Class III–V (±40–50%) estimates and indicative/non-binding — several (Vopak, VTTI) are pre-FEED Letters of Intent / Commercial Information Packages, not firm quotes.
