Clean-molecule screen — indicative only

Two frameworks are in scope: EU RFNBO (RED II/III, ≥70 % GHG reduction, ≈28.2 gCO₂e/MJ derived ceiling, ISCC EU / CertifHy) and Korea KEEI (proposed CI < 4.0 kgCO₂/kgH₂). Conversion used: LHV H₂ ≈ 120 MJ/kg, so 1 kgCO₂/kgH₂ ≈ 8.33 gCO₂/MJ. Only the cracker's direct CI is shown; upstream ammonia CI stacks on top.

A screen against a threshold is not a certification outcome. Qualification needs full additionality, correlation and scheme verification.

Sources, derivations & flagged discrepancies

Every figure is either quoted from the Gentari-ammoniacracker source packages (with section and revision), derived by a stated calculation from those figures, or labelled an assumption. Cost figures are Class IV–V estimates (±40–50 %) and non-binding.

What this tool models

An ammonia cracker converts NH₃ back into hydrogen via the endothermic decomposition 2NH₃ → N₂ + 3H₂ (ΔH ≈ +92.4 kJ/mol at reaction conditions, or the ΔH⁰ ≈ 46 kJ/mol NH₃ basis Casale quotes; the two bases are not interchangeable). The cracked gas is purified by ammonia recovery and PSA to 99.5–99.999 %wt H₂.

Enter the H₂ capacity you need, pick a technology basis and fuel mode, and the model returns the technical sizing (ammonia, fuel, power, CO₂) plus CAPEX from power-law curves fitted to the licensor data, and OPEX and LCOH from your ammonia, electricity and gas prices. The required NH₃ feed is the demand on the receiving terminal. Use “🔗 Link model to storage” to send it to the NH₃ Storage Design module.

  • Single data point: 12 ktpa H₂; NH₃-fired 7.03 t/t, NG-fired 6.83 t/t
  • €47M lump-sum turnkey (±40 %), incl. construction licence fee; €8.50/t H₂ operation fee
  • Licensor/duiker/duiker-johor-hub.md, Budgetary Proposal 122380, 05 Dec 2025
